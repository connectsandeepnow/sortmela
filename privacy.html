Privacy Policy — SortMela

App: SortMela: Hexa Sort Puzzle (com.sortmela.game)
Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version

SortMela does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, no sign-up, and no server that receives anything about you. The game works completely offline.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress — levels completed, stars, coins, and settings such as sound and vibration — using Android's standard local app storage. This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not collect or use your location.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and this version shows no ads.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties, because we hold none.

Children

SortMela is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive Android permissions. It needs no network access to play.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds advertising, analytics, or cloud save, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the Play Store "Data safety" section will be updated to match. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
